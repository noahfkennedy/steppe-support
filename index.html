A little more life beyond the screen.

Steppe is a free focus app for iPhone. Choose distracting apps and websites, start a timed Wander, and make room for reading, rest, and time outside.

Set recurring Block Times, see current-device Screen Time in Journey, and keep private Field Notes on your phone.

The first App Store release is being prepared. No account is required.
