Screen Time access

Allow Steppe to use Screen Time during setup, then choose your apps and websites with Apple's picker. You can revisit setup in Block List → Settings → Run setup again. If permission has been revoked, restore it in iOS Settings.

A blocked app did not become available

Open Steppe so it can check the saved deadline. Check whether another active Block Time or Wander still applies to that app. If the problem continues, tell us the expected end time and what happened.

Journey is unavailable or out of date

Check Screen Time access and use Refresh in Journey. iOS controls report delivery and freshness. Unavailable information does not mean zero usage.

Send a problem report

Open Block List → Settings → Help → Share problem report. You can email the report or share its file. Review it before sending: technical timestamps can reveal when you used a protection feature. Nothing uploads automatically.

Your journal and saved history

This release stores history on your device and has no Steppe account or automatic journal sync. Deleting the app can lose that history. Keep any copy you need and check your Apple backup settings before deleting it.
