Steppe privacy policy

Updated October 26, 2026

Steppe helps you spend less time on your phone. This policy describes the current app: your focus history, journal, settings, and Screen Time information stay on your device by default. Steppe has no account requirement, advertising, or tracking.

Screen Time access

Steppe asks for Apple's individual Screen Time permission when you choose to allow it. You select apps, categories, and websites with Apple's picker. Steppe stores those selections as Apple's opaque tokens and uses them to apply the restrictions you choose.

Journey displays current-device Screen Time, Pickups, and app durations through Apple's Screen Time report extension. Usage values and app identities stay inside that report extension. They are not copied into the main app, diagnostic reports, or a Steppe server. A small in-memory report cache disappears when iOS ends the extension process.

You can revoke Steppe's Screen Time permission in iOS Settings. iOS controls the information and restriction features available to Steppe.

Information stored on your device

Steppe stores your Block Lists and Block Times, preferences, focus-session dates and durations, progress, and the Field Notes you write. A local identifier helps the app maintain its saved history; Steppe does not send it to a server.

The app and its blocking extensions share only the local information they need to apply your chosen restrictions. Steppe does not automatically upload your journal, selections, or usage history, sell your data, or use it for advertising.

Local problem reports

Steppe keeps a bounded technical log on your device to help diagnose protection problems. It contains app and iOS versions, event and error categories, and technical timestamps. Those timestamps can reveal when you used a protection feature. It excludes selected app names and tokens, journal text, account details, and raw Screen Time usage.

Each local log is limited to 200 events. Old events are removed on the next write after seven days, and exported reports exclude events older than seven days. iOS may clear cached logs sooner. Nothing is sent automatically.

Settings lets you prepare and share a problem report. You decide whether to send it and which service to use. Files you save or share are separate copies outside Steppe's control.

When you contact support

If you email support, we receive your email address, the message and attachments you choose to send, and any diagnostic report you include. We use that information to respond and investigate the problem. Please avoid including journal entries or other sensitive information unless you want to share them.

Your email or sharing provider and our email provider process those messages under their own policies. Support information is not used for advertising or sold. You can contact us to request deletion of a support message or report; legal obligations may require us to retain some records.

Backups and deletion

This release has no Steppe account or automatic journal sync. Apple's device backups may include local Steppe data depending on your settings. Apple controls those backups, and restoring local history depends on the backup available to you.

Deleting Steppe removes its local app data and can lose your focus history and journal. Revoke Screen Time access in iOS Settings before deleting the app. Deletion does not remove copies you shared or saved elsewhere, support messages, or existing device backups. Manage those copies with the relevant service.

Links and policy changes

Steppe's photography credits and support links open external services only when you choose them. Those services have their own privacy policies.

We will update this policy when Steppe's handling of information changes. Optional broader access or diagnostic uploads require a clear choice before they occur. The date above identifies this version.
